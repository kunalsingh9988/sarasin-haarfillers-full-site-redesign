## Image provenance

Illustrative lifestyle WebPs are in `assets/women/`. The ImageGen prompts used warm daylight, restrained grading and realistic hair and skin texture. Each prompt directed a distinct composition and avoided logos, medical claims and patient impersonation.

| Asset | Prompt direction |
| --- | --- |
| `hero-*` | A woman with loose brown hair by a softly lit plaster wall, in a landscape editorial portrait. |
| `understanding-*` | A dark-blonde woman examining a lock of hair by a window. |
| `thinning-*` | Close portrait of light-brown hair with a visible centre part. |
| `volume-*` | Side profile of a woman holding her dark ponytail. |
| `hormones-*` | A silver-haired woman in natural window light. |
| `routine-*` | A freckled woman pressing her hands to her cheek during home skincare. |
| `closing-*` | A woman with curly hair looking toward a tall window. |

The authentic results, clinic interior, treatment video, original Karen portrait and real product photography come from the existing Sarasin assets. The follicle diagram is Henry Vandyke Carter's public-domain plate 944, stored as a WebP. [Gray’s Anatomy illustration](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gray944.png).
